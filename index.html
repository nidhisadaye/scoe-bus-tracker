Help & FAQ

How do I track the bus?
Tap Track Bus on the home screen. The live map will show the current bus location updated in real time via GPS.
How do I buy a pass?
Tap Buy Pass on the home screen. Choose Daily (₹10), Monthly (₹49) or Premium (₹99). A QR code will be generated — show it to the driver at boarding.
How do I receive broadcast alerts?
Alerts from drivers appear in Messages (accessible from the side menu). Make sure Notifications are turned ON in your preferences.
What are the bus timings?
Tap Schedules on the home screen. Morning pick-up at 8:30 AM, mid-day at 11:00 AM, evening return at 4:20 PM, and late return at 5:50 PM.
The bus shows FULL — what do I do?
When the driver marks the bus full, the status pill on the Live Map turns red. Contact the transport office via Admin → Call Now for alternate arrangements.
How do I contact support?
Go to Admin on the home screen and tap Call Now to reach the SCOE Transport Department directly at 022-2774-3706.
I logged in as Driver — what extra features do I have?
Drivers get two extra tiles: Controls (start GPS tracking, mark bus full/available) and Broadcast (send live messages to all students via the app).
